# Howard

**Source of truth:** [spirit-clash](https://github.com/kincheungchi0-create/spirit-clash)
`src/game/rig/howard_kit.ts`, `artgen/master_howard.txt`, `public/assets/paint/howard/`, `public/assets/voice/howard/`.

**Look:** Cheerful clever 7yo HK primary-school boy, chibi (~3 heads). Messy fluffy brown hair, big brown eyes, confident grin. White polo with blue-and-yellow trimmed collar + school badge, navy shorts, white socks, blue-and-white sneakers, black wristbands, navy backpack with yellow accents.

**Weapons / kit:** Stationery rush — giant yellow pencil poke, ballpoint-pen sword slash, teal textbook shield (opens), spiral notebook projectile, wooden ruler smash; homework / alphabet storm ultimate (flying pages + letters).

**In this build:** Ikemen KFM720 mechanical base. Idle + portrait copied from spirit-clash paint. Voices copied from spirit-clash Fish takes (`atk1`/`hurt1`/`ko`/`select`/`win1`).
