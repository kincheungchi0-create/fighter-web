# Daddy

**Source of truth:** [spirit-clash](https://github.com/kincheungchi0-create/spirit-clash)
`src/game/rig/daddy_kit.ts`, `artgen/master_daddy.txt`, `public/assets/paint/daddy/`, `public/assets/voice/daddy/`.

**Look:** Howard's food-loving very round dad. Short dark hair, round glasses, moustache, double chin; orange tee stretched over huge belly (sauce stain, belly button peek), khaki shorts, brown sandals.

**Weapons / kit:** Food heavy — chopstick flurry, belly bump, fork launch, dinner-knife, plate block / bao toss, chomp bite; all-you-can-eat food-storm ultimate.

**Fight sprites:** Codex/spirit-clash paint packed into `daddy.sff` (SFF v2 PNG) via `tools/pack_sff_v2.py` — not KFM visuals. Mechanical base remains KFM720 `.air/.cmd/.cns`.
